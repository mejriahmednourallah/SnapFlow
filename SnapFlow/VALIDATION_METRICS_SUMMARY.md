## Report Text To Use

The validation phase was measured from three evidence sources: GitHub/local command output, online Supabase audit history, and a controlled benchmark on three safe public websites. The online Supabase export contained 22 completed audits. Excluding one timestamp anomaly, the average product-level audit completion time was 607.29 seconds, with a minimum of 52 seconds and a maximum of 4491 seconds. These values measure the application-level interval between audit creation and completion persistence, not the internal duration of each microservice phase.

The backend test suites showed stronger stability than the frontend at the time of measurement. The Aggregator passed 110 tests, the NLP worker passed 79 tests with one skipped test, the visual regression service passed 46 tests, and the form executor passed 106 tests with 22 skipped tests. The frontend suite executed 223 tests, with 209 passing, 13 failing and one skipped. This exposes a concrete stabilization task instead of hiding the issue behind a generic statement.

The controlled benchmark confirmed that the local/pre-production audit stack could complete small capped scans and return KPI output for all three targets. With `max_pages=3`, the benchmark processed seven pages in total. All three scans completed, all generated KPI output, and all scanned pages were processed by the NLP worker.

Coverage percentages are intentionally not reported yet because the current measurement run did not produce valid coverage artifacts. The next measurable improvement is to add stable coverage jobs to CI for Vitest, pytest-cov and Go coverage, then report those generated values.
